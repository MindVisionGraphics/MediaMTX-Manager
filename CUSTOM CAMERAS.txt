OTHER RTSP CAMERAS

This cumulative update also includes the previous stream-forwarding feature.
Quit the manager, extract every file into your existing manager folder (replace
matching files), reopen Start Manager.vbs and refresh your existing browser tab.
Keep your existing data and runtime folders.

Under Cameras > Add camera, choose:
- Tapo camera: the existing main-stream / low-bandwidth setup.
- Other RTSP camera: supply the camera IP, port and manufacturer's RTSP path.

For example, if the manufacturer gives rtsp://192.168.1.50:554/Streaming/Channels/101,
enter 192.168.1.50, port 554, and /Streaming/Channels/101 in their separate fields.
Paths can include quality queries such as /cam/realmonitor?channel=1&subtype=0.
Enter username/password separately. Never put passwords or access tokens in the
stream path. Passwords are encrypted for your Windows account. Choose No login
required only when the camera already allows anonymous RTSP viewing.

Choose H.264 video in the camera's own settings for browser compatibility.
Camera discovery, ONVIF setup, H.265 conversion, RTSPS, RTMP and SRT camera inputs
are not included in this addition. A manufacturer's app working does not imply
that its camera offers RTSP; check that camera model's instructions.
