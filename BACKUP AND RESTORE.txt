BACKUP AND RESTORE — SETTINGS UPDATE

INSTALL
Stop any recording, Quit manager, and copy ALL ZIP contents into your existing manager folder.
Replace files; keep the existing data and runtime folders. Open that folder's Start Manager.vbs.
Refresh the manager page, then go to Settings > Backup and restore.
No WordPress, Apache or router changes are needed. Previous app features are included.

CREATE A BACKUP
Choose a passphrase of at least 12 characters and repeat it.
Leave Include camera passwords checked to transfer those passwords to another Windows computer.
Download encrypted backup saves a .mtxbackup.json file through your browser.
Keep the backup somewhere separate from the camera computer, and keep its passphrase safe.
The app cannot recover a forgotten passphrase. Automatic local restore points are not a
replacement for a backup stored somewhere else.

INCLUDED
Workspace name, camera names/addresses/accounts, recurring schedules, recording-storage limits,
and website sharing configuration. Optional camera passwords are inside the encrypted file.
The whole backup is passphrase encrypted, even if camera passwords are omitted.
Camera passwords are unlocked locally for encryption and re-protected for the destination
Windows account when restored. There are no plaintext camera passwords in the downloaded JSON.

NOT INCLUDED
Recorded video, running programs, Windows/Apache/WordPress configuration, activity history,
current manual recording sessions/timers, and browser-only preferences such as animation choice.
Backing up videos separately remains necessary if you want to recover footage after drive loss.

RESTORE
Choose the backup file, enter its passphrase and select Preview backup.
The preview checks the file and lists the setup to be restored; it does not change settings.
It shows which current cameras will be replaced. This version replaces, rather than merges,
the camera/settings setup. Existing recorded video files are not moved or deleted.
Restore these settings asks for confirmation and stops current recordings before applying.

RESTORED CAMERAS ARRIVE PAUSED.
Recording schedules, public sharing and automatic cleanup are restored switched OFF.
Their configured days, times, camera selections and limits are retained for your review.
Resume cameras and enable the automation/sharing options you want after checking them.
If passwords were omitted, edit the restored cameras and enter their Camera Account passwords.
Schedules follow the destination computer's clock and time zone.
Your server keeps its prior running/stopped state; camera feeds remain paused until resumed.

PREVIOUS SETUP
Before applying a restore, the manager saves a local restore point in data/restore-points.
Its saved camera secrets use Windows protection, as they do in workspace.json.
Afterward Settings > Backup and restore shows Preview previous setup.
You can review and restore that configuration too; it also arrives with cameras paused and
sharing/schedules/cleanup switched off. Restoring a previous setup creates another restore point.
These local recovery points remain on this computer and do not include video files.
If applying a restore fails after stopping the server, the app attempts to retain/restart
the old setup automatically. Tests cover a restore-point write failure and rollback.

LIMITS
Backup format version 1 supports up to 100 cameras; files must be under 2 MB.
Restore previews expire after 10 minutes or if the current configuration changes.
Preview the file again if it expires. A used restore preview cannot be applied twice.
Encrypted exports use AES-256-GCM with a random salt/nonce and a fixed scrypt key derivation.
No new external service or account is involved.

CHECKS COMPLETED
Authenticated encrypted round-trip; wrong passphrases, damaged ciphertext, invalid schema,
duplicate/path-traversal camera IDs and altered encryption headers rejected; optional password
omission; credential re-wrapping; preview without changes; stale/reused-preview rejection;
actual Windows credential export/re-encryption, previous-setup recovery and rollback failure test;
saved video bytes preserved; schedule/storage and Standard/Data Saver regression tests;
browser layout and accessible labels. Tests used isolated sample settings, not your live setup.
A restore on another physical computer still needs user testing.
