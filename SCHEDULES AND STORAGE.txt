SCHEDULED RECORDING AND AUTOMATIC STORAGE CLEANUP

INSTALL ON THE WEBSITE/CAMERA COMPUTER
1. Stop any active recording, then Quit manager.
2. Open this update ZIP and copy ALL its contents into the existing manager folder.
   Choose Replace files. Do not leave the update in a separate folder.
   Keep your existing runtime and data folders; neither is included in the small update.
3. Confirm recording-automation.mjs and website-feeds.mjs are beside server.mjs.
4. Open that folder's Start Manager.vbs once, then select Recordings.
5. You should see Recording schedules and Recording storage above the saved videos.

No WordPress, Apache or router changes are needed. Mobile-friendly public video stays enabled
for the cameras you previously selected. Camera passwords and existing recordings are retained.
Schedules and automatic deletion are OFF by default until you save enabled settings.

RECORDING SCHEDULES
Choose a camera's start days, start/end time, Enable scheduled recording, then Save schedule.
One recurring time window per camera is supported in this update.
Times follow the camera computer's Windows clock/time zone, shown on the page.
An earlier end time runs through midnight into the next day. Selected days are the START days.
Example: Monday, 10 PM to 6 AM records Monday night through Tuesday morning.
Start and end times must differ.
The computer must be awake, the manager and MediaMTX server running, and the camera resumed.
Missed hours while the computer was off are not recorded retrospectively.
Schedules are checked approximately every five seconds.

MANUAL CONTROLS
Manual Record continues until you stop it, including outside scheduled hours.
Stop recording inside an active scheduled window skips the remainder of that session.
The next scheduled session can start normally. Editing/saving a schedule clears that skip.
Pausing a camera or stopping the server prevents recording; these controls are not overridden.
When you resume the camera/server, a still-requested manual or currently active scheduled
recording can resume. Saved schedules and manual intent survive manager restart.

STORAGE
Choose recording age, maximum recording space and minimum free drive space.
Use 0 to disable an individual limit. Storage figures labelled GB use 1,073,741,824 bytes.
Defaults shown are 30 days, 50 GB of recordings and 5 GB free, but cleanup starts OFF.
Preview these limits evaluates your entered limits without saving them or deleting anything.
Enabling automatic cleanup and saving starts permanent deletion of eligible old recordings.
The oldest eligible recordings are deleted first. Cleanup checks approximately once a minute.
Only MP4 files inside this manager's data/recordings stream folders are eligible.
Linked folders and files are not followed. Files updated in the last two minutes and the
latest file of an active recorder are protected. Open files may also be held by Windows.
Limits can briefly be exceeded while protected files finish or between checks.

If free space or recording quota cannot be satisfied, recording pauses; live viewing and
public website video continue. Recording can resume when the configured limits allow it.
Run cleanup now uses SAVED limits and asks before deleting the estimated eligible files.
Disabling automatic cleanup also disables this update's configured recording-storage guard.
Deleted recordings are permanently removed; they do not go into the Windows Recycle Bin.
Settings backups and external video backups are not part of this update.

QUICK CHECKS
1. Set a schedule starting shortly, enable it and check that a recording is created.
2. Press Stop during its scheduled session; it should stay stopped for that session.
3. Try Preview these limits; it should report eligible files without removing anything.
4. Enable storage cleanup only after choosing the limits you want.
5. Check that your phone's website video still plays smoothly.
