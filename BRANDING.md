# MediaMTX Manager branding

The owner approved the aligned logo on October 22, 2026.

Use `dist/assets/app-logo.svg` as the vector master. The blue M has equal legs,
the mint play triangle sits on the same baseline, and two broadcast curves share
the M's top alignment. The triangle and broadcast curves use matching horizontal
bounds. Keep the dark navy rounded badge and blue/mint palette.

`Build-Logo.ps1` in the packaging sources exports matching PNG, ICO and ICNS files.
The sidebar, browser favicon, desktop title bar, shortcuts, Windows executable,
installer and Mac bundle must use this approved revision. Older versioned files
are retained only for design history and must not be restored as active assets.
